SLM Recorder v1.48 -> v1.49 OTA acknowledgement reliability

Functional changes:
- config.h: recorder software version 1.48 -> 1.49.
- src/tasks/web_task.cpp: increase the successful OTA HTTP-acknowledgement-to-reboot delay from 500 ms to 2000 ms.
- src/tasks/web_task.cpp: keep support-triggered reboot on its existing explicit 500 ms delay.
- docs/01_recorder_requirements.md: document the 2000 ms OTA reboot acknowledgement delay.
- README.md / VALIDATION.md: document and validate the OTA acknowledgement/reboot behavior and Bridge v0.3.47 pairing.

Rationale:
- The recorder uses ESPAsyncWebServer. request->send(200, "text/plain", "ok") queues the final OTA response asynchronously. Rebooting only 500 ms later can cause a marginal Wi-Fi/TCP connection to lose the final acknowledgement even though the firmware image was successfully written. The 2000 ms delay gives the response more time to reach the phone before Wi-Fi is intentionally dropped by ESP.restart().

Release note:
- Source version is 1.49. No v1.49 compiled firmware binary is included in this source delta; docs/manifest.json remains on the last generated v1.48 image until a v1.49 binary is built and published.
